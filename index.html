What we do

Tweaks that make sense.

Sin City is centered around PC performance. We share and build tweaks aimed at smoother gameplay, lower system overhead, better responsiveness, and cleaner gaming setups without pretending one setting magically fixes every PC.

01 / 04
Performance side

FPS & system tuning

Optimization for people who want their PC focused on the game instead of unnecessary background junk. The goal is a cleaner, more responsive setup while keeping users in control of what gets changed.

Connection side

Latency-focused tuning

Network and configuration guidance aimed at reducing avoidable local latency and keeping a gaming connection clean and consistent.
